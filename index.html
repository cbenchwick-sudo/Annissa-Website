// The Architect

Annissa Neider.

AIA · NCARB

Licensed Architect: OH · WV · PA · FL · GA · Founder, A. Neider Architecture

Annissa found her passion for architecture watching her grandpa build masterful replica furniture in his woodshop. That early love of craft and precision never left her. With over 15 years of experience at both medium and large firms, she brings the expertise of a big firm with the personal attention only a small, focused practice can offer.

She has devoted her career to her hometown of Youngstown, OH and is honored to be part of its downtown renaissance. As founder of a woman-owned firm, Annissa built A. Neider Architecture on a straightforward belief: clients deserve honest guidance, real collaboration, and an architect who stays involved from the first conversation through construction close-out.

Women in Architecture
Having worked in firms of all sizes, our team understands the challenges of supporting women in architecture and construction. A. Neider Architecture holds WBE and EDGE certifications from the Ohio Department of Administrative Services, with federal recognition pending.
Mentorship Matters

// How It Works

The Process

Step 01→

Discovery Call

A real conversation with our team about your site, scope, budget, and timeline — no automated intake, no runaround.

Step 02→

Concept Design

Schematic layouts, space planning, and structural concepts refined with your feedback before committing to full drawings.

Step 03→

Drawings & Permits

Full construction documents and permit filings. We handle the regulatory coordination so your project doesn't stall.

Step 04✓

Construction Support

RFI review, submittal review, site visits, shop drawing review, and final close-out. We stay engaged until the keys are handed over.
